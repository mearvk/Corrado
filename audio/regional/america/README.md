# Audio / Radio — North American (US / Canada) Corrado

Region-specific notes for the **North American** Corrado's factory audio.
For the region-neutral systems and wiring reference, see the parent
[`audio/SYSTEMS.md`](../../SYSTEMS.md) and [`audio/WIRING.md`](../../WIRING.md).

## Head units (North America)

- US/Canada Corrados commonly shipped with **Blaupunkt** single-DIN cassette
  head units (the classic "Frankfurt"/"Bremen"-style decks), or a
  VW-branded equivalent.
- A **trunk-mounted CD changer** was the period-correct upgrade on later US
  cars.
- As in Europe, an **amplified premium system** (external amp + door
  speakers/tweeters) was available on better-equipped cars.

## Tuner / band specifics

- The tuner uses the **North American** band plan: **FM 87.9–107.9 MHz** on
  the US **200 kHz** raster and the **US AM** band — different from Europe's
  step and bands (**no Long Wave**).
- A European head unit mistunes the US band and vice versa; use a
  **US-market** head unit for correct reception on a US car.

## Connector

- Same **VW/ISO** connector family (pre-Quadlock) as the rest of the range —
  see [`audio/WIRING.md`](../../WIRING.md). Aftermarket ISO adapters for the
  **1990–1994 Corrado** (e.g. Metra, Absolute, RED WOLF) target this US
  wiring; amp-integration harnesses (1993-on) suit the premium system.
- VW uses **Brown for ground** and **Red/White for constant +12 V**; US
  cars often lack a true switched/accessory feed at the plug, so installers
  tap the fuse box — confirm before wiring.
