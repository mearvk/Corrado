# Corrado Factory Audio (1990–1996)

Reference notes on the Volkswagen Corrado's **factory stereo systems** and
**audio wiring** for model years 1990–1996 (the Corrado was built from
September 1988 to July 1995; US sales ran 1990–1994, with cars still sold
into 1995, and the 1996 entry here covers the final run-out/other markets).

> This is background/restoration reference, not a VW service manual. Colours
> and fitment varied by **market (US / Europe / UK / California)**, trim and
> build date, and by whether the car had the **standard 4-speaker** system
> or an **amplified/premium** setup. Always verify against your own car and
> the official VW wiring documentation (e.g. a Bentley manual) before
> cutting into the loom.

## Quick summary

- **Head unit:** single-DIN cassette radio. VW's own-brand **"Alpha / Beta /
  Gamma"** units (built by Blaupunkt/Philips) in Europe; **Blaupunkt**
  (Frankfurt/Bremen-style) units commonly fitted to US cars. Later cars
  could have an in-dash or trunk CD changer.
- **Standard system:** head unit + **4 speakers** (dash + rear), head-unit
  power only, no separate amplifier.
- **Premium system:** adds larger/door speakers, **tweeters**, and on some
  cars an **external power amplifier** (the "Sound System" option), fed from
  the head unit's pre-amp/line-level or speaker outputs.
- **Connector:** the Corrado uses the **old-style VW/ISO radio connectors**
  of the late-'80s/early-'90s (chamfered multi-pin power + speaker blocks,
  commonly adapted to standard ISO). It is **not** the later 40-pin
  "Quadlock" connector used from the late 1990s on.
