# Corrado Factory Stereo Systems — Standard vs. Premium (1990–1996)

## 1. Factory head units

In this era Volkswagen fitted **single-DIN cassette radios**. Two naming
worlds overlap depending on market:

- **Europe/UK:** VW-branded **"Alpha", "Beta" and "Gamma"** radios. These
  were VW's own line (manufactured for VW by **Blaupunkt** and **Philips**),
  rising from basic (Alpha) to feature-rich (Gamma). Most were coded
  (anti-theft code required after battery disconnect).
- **North America:** Corrados were commonly delivered with **Blaupunkt**
  head units (the classic "Frankfurt"/"Bremen"-style single-DIN cassette
  decks), sometimes a VW-branded equivalent.

Typical head-unit features by tier:

| Tier | Typical unit | Features |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Base/standard | Alpha / basic Blaupunkt | AM/FM, cassette, 4-speaker output, ~4×20 W internal amp |
| Mid | Beta | AM/FM, cassette, Dolby, better tuner, pre-amp outputs on some |
| Premium | Gamma / amplified "Sound System" | AM/FM, cassette, Dolby, more power or external amp feed, often with a CD changer provision |

A **CD changer** (in-dash slot was rare; a **trunk-mounted multi-disc
changer** controlled by the head unit was the period-correct upgrade) could
be dealer- or factory-optioned on later cars.

> Many European cars require the radio's **anti-theft code** to be entered
> after any loss of power. Keep the code card; without it the unit locks.

## 2. Standard vs. premium — what you got

### Standard (4-speaker)
- Head unit powered the speakers directly from its **internal amplifier**
  (roughly 4 × 15–25 W).
- Speaker count: **4** — a pair in the **dash** and a pair in the **rear
  side panels**.
- No external amplifier, no dedicated tweeters (or only simple ones).

### Premium / "Sound System"
- Added **front door** or larger speakers and dedicated **tweeters** for a
  component-style front stage.
- On so-equipped cars, an **external power amplifier** (often mounted in the
  trunk/boot area or behind trim) drove the speakers; the head unit fed it
  line-level or high-level signal.
- Result: more output and a fuller stage; wiring includes an **amp
  turn-on/remote** and separate amp power feed (see `WIRING.md`).

> Not every Corrado has the amplified system. Presence of a trunk amp, extra
> speaker runs, or a factory sub indicates the premium setup. If an
> aftermarket "Bose/amp integration" harness (e.g. Metra 70-1787, listed for
> VW/Audi 1993-on) is referenced for your car, it had an amplified system.

## 3. Speaker sizes & locations

Factory speaker fitment for the Corrado (verify your car — door vs. dash
fitment and sizes vary by system and market):

| Location | Typical size | Role |
|----------|--------------|------|
| **Dashboard** (corners, firing up) | **3.5 in** | Front fill / highs |
| **Front doors** (premium / some markets) | ~**5.25 in** (door pod) | Front midbass |
| **Rear side panels / quarter** | **4 × 6 in** | Rear fill |
| **Tweeters** (premium) | small, dash/A-pillar/sail | Highs |

- A **standard** car is essentially **dash 3.5" + rear 4×6"**.
- A **premium** car adds **door speakers and tweeters**, and often the amp.

## 4. Year-by-year notes (1990–1996)

| Year(s) | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| **1990–1991** | G60 era. Base cars: Alpha/basic Blaupunkt + 4 speakers. Premium "Sound System" optional on better-equipped cars. Old-style VW/ISO radio connectors. |
| **1992** | VR6 introduced alongside G60. Equipment broadly similar; premium amplified system more commonly specified on VR6/high trims. |
| **1993–1994** | VR6 era (US final years were 1994). Gamma-class radios and amplified systems more common; CD changer provision on some. Harness fitment references begin to list "amp integration" (1993-on). |
| **1995** | Final European production year (build ended July 1995). Similar systems; last-of-line radios. |
| **1996** | Run-out / later-registered and other-market cars. No fundamentally new factory audio architecture for the Corrado; still the pre-Quadlock VW/ISO wiring. |

> The Corrado never moved to the late-1990s **Quadlock** radio system; it
> kept the earlier VW/ISO connector family for its whole life.
